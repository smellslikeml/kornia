`focal_frequency_loss` and `FocalFrequencyLoss` compare a prediction and a target in the 2-D DFT domain, weighting each frequency's squared error by its own magnitude so that the frequencies a prediction currently gets hardest dominate the loss. The weight is detached, half-precision inputs are transformed in float32, and the image can be split into a `patch_factor`-by-`patch_factor` grid of patches compared spectrum by spectrum.
